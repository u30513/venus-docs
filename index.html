Buying & selling

Buy

Paste a token address anywhere in the chat and Venus opens a token card: live price, market size, liquidity, and warnings if something looks dangerous. Tap an amount (0.1, 0.5, 1 SOL or a custom one) and the swap executes.

Sell

Tap Sell, pick the token, choose 25% / 50% / 75% / 100% or a custom share. Venus asks you to confirm, then sends the swap.

Your receipt

Every trade returns a receipt: what moved, what you received, and a View on Solscan link so you can verify it on the public blockchain. Sell receipts also show the minimum guaranteed — the least the swap was allowed to deliver under your slippage setting; if the price had moved further than that, the trade would have failed rather than sell cheaper.

Smart Trade

Smart Trade is designed for a simple idea: you choose the token and the strategy, and Venus takes care of managing the trade.

You can use one of the predefined strategies or configure your own. Once the trade starts:

  1. Venus buys the token you selected.
  2. If the price goes up, Venus gradually sells part of the position at the different profit targets, locking in profits along the way.
  3. A stop-loss protects the position if the price moves down. As profit targets are reached, the stop-loss also moves up to protect the progress already made.
  4. Once the last profit target is reached, the remaining tokens stay in the trade with a trailing stop, allowing the position to continue if the price keeps rising.
  5. Whenever something important happens, Venus sends you a message so you always know how the trade is progressing.

You can also check the position at any time and see what has already been collected and what is still in the trade — this is what that screen looks like:

FAQ

A token shows "unavailable" — is my money gone?
Not at all — your tokens are exactly where you left them. "Unavailable" only means the live price could not be fetched at that second, usually because the price service was busy. Tap Refresh and the numbers come back.
What if the price service goes down during my Smart Trade?
Venus tells you right away that monitoring is delayed — and, most importantly, it touches nothing while it cannot see prices. It keeps retrying in the background and messages you the moment everything is back to normal.
What happens if Venus restarts?
You won't even notice. Wallets, settings and positions are saved continuously, and a running Smart Trade picks up its monitoring again by itself after any restart.
I have a token I can't sell. What now?
It happens in the meme world: some tokens lose their entire market ("rugged"), and once nobody is buying, there is no way to sell — on any platform, not just Venus. What Venus can do is help you tidy up: hide the token from your lists with 🙈 Hide / show tokens, and recover the locked SOL deposits with 🧹 Clean up wallet.
Why is a small amount of SOL "locked" for each token?
That's simply how Solana works: every token you hold lives in its own small account, and each account keeps about 0.002 SOL as a deposit while it exists. Nothing is lost — when you fully sell a token, 🧹 Clean up wallet closes the empty account and puts that SOL back in your wallet.
Is this financial advice?
No — and be careful with anyone who promises otherwise. Venus executes your decisions with discipline and keeps you informed; it does not predict markets. Meme tokens are extremely risky and can go to zero, so trade only what you can afford to lose.
