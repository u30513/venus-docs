Buying & selling

Buy

Paste a token address anywhere in the chat and Venus opens a token card: live price, market size, liquidity, and warnings if something looks dangerous. Tap an amount (0.1, 0.5, 1 SOL or a custom one) and the swap executes.

Sell

Tap Sell, pick the token, choose 25% / 50% / 75% / 100% or a custom share. Venus asks you to confirm, then sends the swap.

Your receipt

Every trade returns a receipt: what moved, what you received, and a View on Solscan link so you can verify it on the public blockchain. Sell receipts also show the minimum guaranteed — the least the swap was allowed to deliver under your slippage setting; if the price had moved further than that, the trade would have failed rather than sell cheaper.

Smart Trade

A Smart Trade is a position that manages itself: you choose the token and the strategy, Venus executes it. It buys, then watches the market around the clock:

  • Take-profits — sells a slice at each target, so gains become real SOL in your wallet.
  • Dynamic stop-loss — starts below your entry and moves up every time a take-profit hits. After the first target, the trade can no longer lose money.
  • Trailing stop — after the last target, the rest rides the trend and sells only when the price falls back a set distance from its highest point.

Your live position always shows the full strategy and your money, clearly separated into what is already collected (safe in your wallet) and what is still in the trade:

FAQ

A token's value shows "unavailable" — is my money gone?
No. It means the live price could not be fetched at that moment (usually a busy price service). Your tokens are untouched — tap Refresh and the value comes back.
What happens to my Smart Trade if the price service goes down?
Venus tells you clearly that monitoring is delayed, keeps your position untouched (it never sells blind), retries continuously, and resumes on its own — with a message — when data returns.
What happens if the bot restarts?
Nothing is lost. Positions, wallets, settings and running Smart Trades are saved continuously; a Smart Trade resumes monitoring automatically after any restart.
I hold a token that cannot be sold. Why?
Some tokens lose all their market liquidity ("rugged") — there is no route to sell them, on any platform. Use 🙈 Hide / show tokens to remove them from your lists, and 🧹 Clean up wallet to reclaim the small SOL deposits locked by empty token accounts.
Why do I see a small SOL deposit "locked" per token?
On Solana, every token you hold needs its own small account, which keeps about 0.002 SOL as a deposit while it exists. When you fully sell a token, 🧹 Clean up wallet closes the empty account and returns that SOL to you.
Is Venus financial advice?
No. Meme tokens are extremely risky and can go to zero. Venus executes your decisions and protects you from avoidable mistakes — it does not predict markets. Trade only what you can afford to lose.
